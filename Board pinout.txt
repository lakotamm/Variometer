Pressure sensors - I2C:
GPIO03 - SIO - Interrupt 3 (alt5)
GPIO04 - SIO - Interrupt 2 (alt5)
GPIO05 - SIO - Interrupt 1 (alt5)
GPIO06 - I2C1 SDA (alt3)
GPIO07 - I2C1 SCL (alt3)

BLE - UART:

GPIO12- UART0 TX (alt2) 
GPIO13 - UART0 RX (alt2)

GPS - UART: (reserved for future)
GPIO20 - UART1 TX (alt2)
GPIO21 - UART1 RX (alt2)

Screen - SPI: (reserved for future)
GPIO16 - SPI0 RX (alt1)
GPIO17 - SPI0 CSn (alt1)
GPIO18 - SPI0 SCK (alt1)
GPIO19 - SPIO TX (alt1)

Audio PWM: (probably only 1 needed)
SPIO22 - PWM3 A (alt3) R  
SPIO23 - PWM3 B (alt3) L

Notes:
MCU - Raspberry Pi RP2350B (QFN-80) Pinout
Pins available on headers - GPIO0 - GPIO25, GPIO40_ADC0 - GPIO43_ADC3

Reserved peripherals:
I2C0 - Barometer
SPI1 - IMU
